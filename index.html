Private personal utility

Know which messages need action—without changing your mailbox.

Personal Inbox Reminder reviews recent Gmail threads with read-only access, identifies messages that may require the operator’s attention, and sends a concise reminder to a private Discord destination.

Read-only Gmail access No public sign-ups No mail modification

Purpose

A narrow reminder workflow

The utility exists for one purpose: reducing the chance that its operator overlooks an email containing a deadline, direct request, account action, interview preparation item, or other necessary follow-up.

  1. Read recent inbox activity The app retrieves recent message headers and limited message text through the Gmail API using gmail.readonly.
  2. Classify for required action Relevant thread content is processed by the operator’s locally configured Hermes Agent and AI service to decide whether a reminder is warranted.
  3. Notify privately Only a concise action-oriented reminder is delivered to the operator’s private Discord destination.

Boundaries

What this utility does not do

Does not send email
The app cannot compose or send messages.
Does not alter Gmail
It cannot mark messages read, change labels, archive, or delete mail.
Does not sell or advertise
Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build marketing profiles.
Does not replace source mail
Reminders may be incomplete or delayed. The operator must verify important details in Gmail.

Data path

Designed for a single operator

OAuth credentials and queue state are stored on the operator’s computer. Limited Gmail content is provided to the configured AI processor for classification, and reminder text is sent to Discord. Details, retention, and revocation instructions are described in the Privacy Policy.

日本語概要
